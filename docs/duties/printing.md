## The four things to check on a Ricoh copier

For every Ricoh copier there are **four things to check**: the Octopus light, the
Octopus and copier screens, paper jams, and the paper level.

### 1. Octopus light

The light must be **steady green**.

![The Octopus reader light on a Ricoh copier](/images/printer-guide/octopus-light.jpg)
*The Octopus light — steady green is the only acceptable state*

::: warning This one you cannot fix
If the light is not steady green there is nothing you can do at the machine —
**report it to ITSC**.
:::

### 2. Octopus screen and copier screen

There must be **no errors** on the displays or on the touch panels.

![The Octopus reader screen](/images/printer-guide/octopus-screen.jpg)
*The Octopus reader screen*

![The Ricoh copier touch panel](/images/printer-guide/copier-panel.jpg)
*The copier touch panel*

Try to clear whatever is reported by **following the instructions shown on the
display or touch panel**. For difficult problems you cannot fix, report to ITSC.

### 3. Paper jam

A jam is shown on the **touch panel**, with the location and the steps to clear
it. Work through them.

::: danger Move the toner out and take the steel stick out
Clearing a jam inside the machine means **taking the toner cartridge out first**
and **removing the steel stick**. Both are marked in the photo below.
:::

![Clearing a paper jam — the toner cartridge and the steel stick](/images/printer-guide/paper-jam-toner.jpg)
*The toner cartridge (upper box) and the steel stick (lower box)*

### 4. Paper level, and refilling

**If a tray is at 50% or below, it needs a refill.**

#### Check the level without pulling the trays out

1. Press **Check Status** on the touch panel.

   ![Check Status on the Ricoh panel](/images/printer-guide/check-status.jpg)
   *Step 1 — Check Status*

2. Press **Maintnc./Inquiry/Mach Info**.

   ![Maintnc./Inquiry/Mach Info on the Ricoh panel](/images/printer-guide/maintnc-inquiry.jpg)
   *Step 2 — Maintnc./Inquiry/Mach Info*

3. The **paper tray status** appears on the right: level and paper size for
   Tray 1, Tray 2, Tray 3 and the Bypass Tray, with the remaining toner band on
   the left.

   ![The paper tray status panel](/images/printer-guide/paper-tray-status.jpg)
   *Step 3 — per-tray level and paper size*

::: tip Seat the tray first
Push each tray fully in until it clicks. The status screen only shows the true
remaining paper once the tray is seated properly.
:::

#### Loading Tray 1 correctly

Tray 1 is a **tandem tray**: you load the left side, and the machine moves the
paper across to the right side by itself.

**Never put paper into the right side of Tray 1 by hand.**

![The right side of Tray 1, marked with a red cross](/images/printer-guide/tray1-right-forbidden.jpg)
*The right side of Tray 1 — never load this by hand*

**Make sure the lever is always at the leftmost position.**

![The tray lever at the leftmost position](/images/printer-guide/tray1-lever-correct.jpg)
*Correct — the lever pushed fully to the left*

![The tray lever not at the leftmost position](/images/printer-guide/tray1-lever-wrong.jpg)
*Wrong — this position still fails; it is not at the leftmost*

**Separate the paper at all 4 sides** before loading — fan the stack so the
sheets are not stuck together.

![Fanning a stack of paper](/images/printer-guide/fan-paper.jpg)
*Separate the stack at all four sides*

**Put the new paper below the old paper**, and load **only the left side**.

![Loading the left side of Tray 1](/images/printer-guide/tray1-load-left.jpg)
*New paper underneath, left side only*

**Do not fill higher than the red mark.**

![The red fill mark inside Tray 1](/images/printer-guide/tray1-red-mark.jpg)
*The red fill limit*

The machine then **moves the paper across to the right side automatically**.

![Paper moving to the right side of Tray 1](/images/printer-guide/tray1-auto-shift.jpg)
*The stack is relocated to the right side on its own*

Once it has, **put paper into the left side again**.

![Loading the left side of Tray 1 a second time](/images/printer-guide/tray1-load-left-again.jpg)
*Refill the left side after the stack has moved across*

### Check the panel again before you leave

If you fixed anything or refilled paper, **go back to the displays and touch
panels** and confirm there are no further errors.

![Re-checking the copier touch panel](/images/printer-guide/recheck-panel.jpg)
*Last step — confirm the panel is clear*

## Toner

- Replace the toner when it is **empty**.
- Read the level on the **Maintnc./Inquiry/Mach Info** screen — it shows
  K / C / M / Y as percentage bands.
- If any toner is **under 20% – 30%**, take a **photo of the status screen** and
  report it on the form, so the service provider can replace it promptly.

![The Machine Information screen](/images/printer-machine-info.jpg)
*Machine Information — toner bands and per-tray paper levels*

## Octopus card jam

A pair of pliers is kept for this: one in **SEKG02 copier 01, Tray 2**, and
another in the **NAB206A B&W copier, Tray 2**. Pliers are available at
**MB412 / SEKG02 / NAB206A**.

1. **First, shut the copier down and restart it.** The card is often ejected
   automatically after the reboot.
2. If that fails, **remove the card with the pliers**.
3. **Wrap the card in thick cloth first** — it protects the card and gives you
   grip.
4. **Warn the user that the card may be damaged** before you start pulling.

::: tip The card reader has its own power switch
Turning **the card reader's power off, waiting 1 minute, then turning it back
on** also works: the reader runs a self-diagnosis and ejects the card safely.
:::

## Scanning

- **Free scanning** — scanning to an email address — is available on **all
  copiers in our computer labs**.
- Use the copier's **built-in Scanning function**.

Users ask about this at the counter, so it is worth knowing which machines can do
it.

## What to refill, and when

| Consumable | Refill when |
| --- | --- |
| **Ink / toner** | It is empty, or the printer **prompts that it is running low** |
| **Paper** | A tray is at **50% or below** |

## Paper Stock Check

::: tip First session only
**Only the first printer check session of the day performs the paper stock
check.** The form asks this directly as a Yes/No question. The ITSC guidelines
single out the **first session on Mondays and Fridays**; it is not expected on
weekend shifts.
:::

Count the remaining **A4 and A3 paper boxes** in the **MB402 paper store** and
record how many are left. Know where the boxes are kept and what the packaging
looks like before your first session.

::: tip If you run out of time
If paper is low but you cannot finish refilling within your session, **mark it on
the submission form** so the next student helper picks it up. That handover note
is the point of the question.
:::
