## The checklist items

Questions 4–26 are answered **Ok** / **Not ok** unless noted.

### 4. Lamp

*Lighting in the Lecture Theatre / Classroom?* — turn on all the lights when you
enter and confirm they work. Report broken lights.

### 5. Yellow Label

Yellow labels must be present on: **1. Computer, 2. Presentation Monitor,
3. Secondary Monitor**.

::: warning Students peel these off
Missing yellow labels are usually deliberate removal, not wear. Report them.
:::

### 6. Teacher Lectern Manuals

**All 3 instruction manuals — 1. PC, 2. AV, 3. Hotline — stuck on the lectern?**

### 7. PC

*Can power on?*

### 8. Teacher Podium Monitor

*Colour? Sharp?*

### 9. Classroom Display Equipment

*Projector / TV Wall / Interactive Panel.* Whichever the room has.

::: danger Display mode
Not set to **PC Screen Only** or **Second Screen Only** — it must be **Extend**
or **Duplicate**. This is the single most common fault found.
:::

### 10. PC Login

*Can login with your own username?* Powering on is not enough — log in as well.

### 11. Keyboard

*Missing keys?* `F4` and `F7` have gone missing before.

### 12. Mouse

*Smooth? Left click ok? Right click ok?*

### 13. CD-Rom

*Can read the data from CD?* — **Ok / Not ok / N/A**. LCH209 uses it for IELTS
and password tests, and some teachers still teach from CDs.

### 14. Speaker Sound Test

The form links a YouTube clip to play. Sound audible = Ok.

### 15. Wired Microphone Sound Test

### 16. Gooseneck Microphone Sound Test

### 17. Wireless Infrared / Digital Hand-held Microphone Sound Test

*Digital hand-held microphone will put on teacher Podium or table.*

### 18. Desk

### 19. Chair

::: tip Broken furniture
**Stick an A4 paper on it marked "Out of Service"** if it is broken.
:::

### 20. Webcam Facing Teacher

### 21. Webcam Facing Students

::: tip Test cameras in Zoom
Use **Zoom → Settings → Video**, not the Windows Camera app — the built-in app
freezes when switching cameras. Also check the **webcam lens cover** is open; a
covered lens gets misreported as a dead camera.
:::

### 22. Tripod for Webcams

*Including Webcam Facing Teacher and Webcam Facing Students.* Steady, head
working, aimed at the students. Some rooms have no tripod.

### 23. Control Panel

Buttons respond, volume adjustable, and not muted or turned right down. The form
includes the "Using the Control AV Control System" reference cards.

### 24. Visualizer Check Result

Open it and switch inputs.

### 25. Remark

*Simple Description Other Problem **With Room Number**.* Always include the room
number — a description without one is not actionable.

### 26. Work-Evidence (non-anonymous)

*Login Teacher PC and Camera (either one classroom).*

Upload a photo. Limits: **10 files, 10 MB each**; Word, Excel, PPT, PDF, image,
video and audio accepted. The photo should show the time and the room, not just
you.

## Before you leave each room

- **Shut down** the teacher PC.
- **Shut down the projector and visualizer**, and turn off the control panel.
- **Leave one light on.**
