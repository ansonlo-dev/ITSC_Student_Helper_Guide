## What to check

Work from the **Classroom Equipment List** spreadsheet, linked at the top of the
checklist form — it lists what each room is supposed to have. Then confirm:

- The AV system powers on from the touch panel
- Each input source selects correctly (Desktop, Notebook, …)
- The teacher PC boots and logs in
- The projector or display panel shows an image
- Sound works
- The microphone works — see which type belongs in the room under
  [Duties Overview](/duties/)

## Microphones

- **Infra-red Wireless Mic** — Lecture Room, Tutorial Room, Computer Lab, and
  old Lecture Theatres with a drawer storing the mic (open with the cabinet key
  in the duty bag).
- **Digital Wireless Mic** — new Lecture Theatres with a mic on the teacher
  podium or in a drawer.

Change batteries from the AA pair in the duty bag when a mic is weak, and fit a
fresh cover from the three types supplied.
