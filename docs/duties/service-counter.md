| | Counter service hours |

## What the duty covers

1. Handle user enquiries via **phone call or walk-in**
2. **Equipment on loan** — see [Equipment on Loan](/duties/av-equipment)
3. Take voicemail, receive deliveries, and keep the counter tidy

Typical enquiry topics: service hotline, user account, on loan, network, 2FA,
and other ad-hoc issues.

### Taking a call

Before you start troubleshooting, get the details you will need for the form:
**name, email, ID, extension or phone number, department and location**. You
cannot fill in the record afterwards from memory.

::: tip Urgent vs non-urgent
Sort the call first. A class already running with no projector is urgent and
goes to the duty staff now. A general "how do I…" question can be answered at
the counter or logged for follow-up. Say which it is when you pass it on.
:::

## Recording enquiries

Mark down **each** encountered user enquiry and submit it via the
**Issue Received by ITSC Student Helper** form:

**<https://forms.office.com/r/30WbQbDCSF>**

| # | Question | What to be careful about |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Student Helper Name** | You |
| 2 | **Staff/Student Name** | Ask for the spelling rather than guessing |
| 3 | **Staff/Student Email** | The route staff will use to follow up — confirm it back to them |
| 4 | **Staff/Student ID** | |
| 5 | **Staff/Student Extension/Phone Number** | For a phone enquiry, get a call-back number before you start |
| 6 | **Staff/Student Department** | |
| 7 | **Staff/Student Location** | Room number, not "the lab upstairs" |
| 8 | **Issue Summary** | **At most 100 characters** — write it as a one-line title |
| 9 | **Issue Detail** | **At least 20 characters** — symptom, error message word for word, and what you already tried |
| 10 | **Status** | See below |

**Status options:**

- Followed up by **User Services Team** Staff
- Followed up by **AV Team** Staff
- **Fixed by Student Helper**
- **Not fixed**

::: warning Pick the status honestly
"Not fixed" is a valid, useful answer. Marking something fixed when the user
walked away unhappy loses the follow-up entirely.
:::

::: tip Ask when unsure
Ask full-time staff if there is anything you are uncertain of.
:::

## Equipment loan at the counter

- **Always press Check-Out** in the system — the loan is not recorded otherwise.
- **Check equipment on return**, item by item: does the wireless presenter still
  have its USB receiver, does the notebook power on, are all accessories back?
- Know **what each item looks like and where it is stored**, and which
  **microphone type** the user needs.
- Use the **barcode reader** rather than typing item numbers.

Full procedure on [Equipment on Loan](/duties/av-equipment).

## Deliveries

For deliveries requiring a signature, go inside and find a staff member — do not
sign for ITSC goods yourself.

## Other counter tasks

- **Helper badges** — hand out and collect; they live in the cabinet.
- **Counter notebook** — the handover log; read it and add to it.
- Know **how to direct people to the Human Resources Office**; it is a common
  question at the counter.
- `Alt`+`R` logs off the counter workstation.

### Unclaimed items

Items handed in at the counter are commonly **HKID cards, student cards, and
wallets or purses**. Log them and hand them to staff — do not leave valuables
sitting on the counter.

## Counter tips

1. Listen carefully to the other person's questions to understand their **true
   situation**.
2. Pay attention to the **speed and tone** of your own speech.
3. Assess the **urgency** of the matter.
4. When seeking help from ITSC colleagues, first clarify whether the enquiry is
   **in person or over the phone**.

More on this in [Talking to Users](/tips/communication).
