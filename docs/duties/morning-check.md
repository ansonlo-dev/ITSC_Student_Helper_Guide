# Morning Check

**Mon – Fri, 08:30 – 10:30.**

## Why it exists

Originally only two SEK rooms needed a morning check. It has since expanded to
many more rooms, concentrating on the **newer rooms** (LBY ground floor, several
LKK rooms) — informally, the newer rooms fail more often. Older rooms are
sampled at random.

The goal is to make sure everything in the room works **before the first class**,
so teachers do not have to phone in a complaint.

::: warning Submit before 9:00 am
The report should be submitted **before 9:00 am, and by 9:30 am at the latest** —
the first class starts at 9:30.
:::

## How much you cover

Each Morning Check session is **3 rooms in 2 hours**:

- About **15 minutes per room** — 3 rooms is roughly 45 minutes
- The remaining time is for filling in the report

The two original SEK slots are the exception; every other slot is a group of 3
rooms.

## Checklist items, and what to watch for

### Room status

Options are **Checked** / **In Use** / **Locked**.

::: warning "In use" should be rare
You are supposed to arrive before the room is occupied. Repeatedly reporting
"in use" means the check is being done too late.
:::

### Lamp — lighting in the room

Report any broken light. MBG11, for example, has a history of tripping its
circuit breaker.

### Manuals

**All 3 instruction manuals** (1. PC, 2. AV, 3. Hotline) must be present and
stuck on the lectern.

### PC

Confirm the machine can **power on _and_ log in** — both are required, one is not
enough.

- If login fails, a missing **network connection** is a common cause. This needs
  resolving immediately.
- **Restart the PC** after testing.

### Keyboard

Press at least a few keys — `A` `B` `C` `D` `E`. Check for dirt and for missing
keycaps (an `F4` key has gone missing before).

### Mouse

Left click, right click and scroll wheel all work, with no stickiness.

### Speaker sound test

Play the audio test; if you hear sound it is OK. **Also test from the computer
itself**, not only the AV source.

### Podium monitor

Confirm it displays correctly.

### Display equipment (projector / display panel)

::: danger Watch the display mode
Do **not** leave it on **PC Screen Only** or **Second Screen Only**. It should be
set to **Extend** or **Duplicate**. Check the display setting shown in the
bottom-right corner.
:::

### Microphone sound test

Switch the mic on; if you hear sound it is OK. Rooms with an **IR mic** must have
that tested too. Morning Check has caught silent mics in LBY and LKKG01 before
they affected a class — this item earns its place.

### Camera

Test **both** directions — **Facing Student** and **Facing Teacher**.

::: tip Use Zoom, not the Windows Camera app
Test with **Zoom → Settings → Video**, because the built-in Windows Camera app
freezes when switching between cameras. In Zoom you can see both the C930 and
C925 cameras. Paul has posted a demonstration video in the group.
:::

- MBY201 and the newer rooms use a different camera model from the older rooms.
- The LKK new-build cameras are fixed to the curtain wall and have **no tripod**.

### Tripod

Confirm it stands steady, the head works, and it is **facing the students**.
Some rooms have no tripod — mark **"No Tripod"**.

### Control panel

Buttons respond and volume is adjustable.

::: warning Order matters
The **control panel and PC must both be OK first** — otherwise you cannot
meaningfully test the microphone or the other equipment.
:::

### Visualizer

Open it and switch inputs to test.

## Work evidence (required)

Morning Check requires a **photo** as work evidence, and the field is
**mandatory** on this form.

::: warning Why it is mandatory here
The requirement was introduced after a helper signed in and went for breakfast.
It is not there to distrust you — it exists because the problem recurred.
:::

The photo must show the **time** and the **surrounding environment**, not just a
selfie. A good pattern: photograph the camera while it is switched on, yourself,
and the state of the room.

ITSC can read login records, but only asks for one photo as practical proof.

## Submit the result

Open **→ Morning Check ←** from the checklist links page and submit the
**Morning Check** form. Question 1 is your name, question 2 is the room group.

::: info Later pages of this form
The form runs to 4 pages; only the first is documented here. The remaining pages
follow the same per-item pattern as the
[Lecture Room Checklist](/duties/lecture-room-check#the-checklist-items).
:::

Report anything broken — a dead light, a silent mic — in the group chat
immediately, as well as on the form.

You cannot sign out until the form is submitted.
