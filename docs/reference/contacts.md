## Escalate immediately when

- A whole lab, building, or service is down
- Anything involving account permissions, security, or a suspected compromise
- Possible data loss
- Damaged, missing, or stolen equipment
- A user who is angry, threatening, or wants to file a complaint
- Anything you have not been trained to do

## Escalate after 15 minutes when

- You have made no real progress on a single user's problem
- A queue is building while you work on one long case
- The fix would require a change you are not authorised to make

## What to include when you escalate

1. **Who** — the user's name and contact, and whether they are still waiting.
2. **Where** — room number, machine hostname or asset number.
3. **What** — the symptom and the exact error message.
4. **Scope** — one user, one machine, or everyone.
5. **Tried** — what you have already done, and what changed as a result.
6. **Urgency** — is a class or a deadline being blocked right now?
