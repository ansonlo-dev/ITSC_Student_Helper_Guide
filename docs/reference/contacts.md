## ITSC duty staff

For everyday questions during counter duty, **seek help from the duty staff
first, then others**. The Duty and Service Counter Roster is posted at the
counter; seating plans are on [Counter Duty](/duties/service-counter).

## Escalate immediately when

- A whole lab, room, building or service is down
- Anything involving account permissions, security, or a suspected compromised
  account
- Possible data loss
- Damaged, missing or stolen equipment
- A user who is angry, threatening, or wants to file a complaint
- Anything you have not been trained to do

## What to include when you escalate

1. **Who** — the user's name and contact, and whether they are still waiting.
2. **Where** — room number, machine name or asset number.
3. **What** — the symptom and the exact error message.
4. **Scope** — one user, one machine, or everyone.
5. **Tried** — what you have already done, and what changed as a result.
6. **Urgency** — is a class or a deadline blocked right now?
7. **Channel** — say whether the user is in person or on the phone.
