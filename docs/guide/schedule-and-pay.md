# Work Hours & Salary

## Work hours

- **Maximum working hours: 16.5 hours per week** (basically 13 hours).
- Anything **more than 15 minutes** early, late, or overtime — let the managers
  know.
- Work hours will be adjusted based on performance.
- The **duty booking system enforces the limit itself**: it blocks a booking that
  would take you past **16 hours** in a week, and it blocks overlapping bookings
  outright. Only Paul can override the limit.

### Why the cap is 16.5 hours

The number comes from the Employment Ordinance's **"continuous contract"** test.
Since **18 January 2026** the Employment (Amendment) Ordinance 2025 has replaced
the old **"418" rule** (18 hours a week for 4 straight weeks) with the **"468"
rule**. You are on a continuous contract once you have been employed by the same
employer for **4 weeks or more** and either:

- worked **17 hours or more in each** of those weeks, or
- worked **68 hours or more in total** across any week plus the three weeks
  before it.

A continuous contract turns a job into the full-benefit kind — rest days, paid
statutory holidays, paid annual leave, sickness allowance, end-of-year payment,
severance and long service payment. A student helper post is not set up that way,
so the roster is kept below both thresholds:

| Rule | Threshold | Student helper cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hours in any single week | 17 hours | **16.5 hours** |
| Hours in any 4-week window | 68 hours | 16.5 x 4 = **66 hours** |

Half an hour a week is the entire margin. That is why the booking system stops
you at 16 hours rather than trusting you to count, and why only Paul can
override it. Details:
[Labour Department — Revise the "Continuous Contract" Requirement](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/news/EAO2025.htm).

## Salary

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly cutoff | Around the **6th to 8th** (cutoff on Saturday) |
| Claim form sent to your student email | The **following Monday** |
| You print and submit it | **Within two days** |
| Payment | The **20th** of each month |

::: warning Submit the claim form within two days
Miss the two-day window and the claim rolls over — you will have to wait until
the next month to be paid for those hours.
:::

The form is the University's **Hourly Pay and Piecework Employee Claim Form**.
Scan the completed and signed copy, send it to `hourlyclaim@LN.edu.hk`, and keep
the paper copy.

Points to watch when filling it in:

- Your name must be **exactly the same** as on your identity card, passport and
  bank account.
- Bank name and account are needed for a first-time claim or when your bank
  details change.
- Department/Unit is **ITSC**; Position Employed is **Student Helper**.

### The CAMP claim form, step by step

Around the **6th to 8th of each month**, Paul pre-fills your worked hours on the
CAMP form. Your part:

1. **Verify the hours** against your own work records.
2. **Fill in the yellow-highlighted fields by hand** — **Student ID**,
   **Bank Account**, **Signature** and **Date**. The Finance Office (FO) requires
   all four; a form missing one comes back to you.
3. **Submit the signed form** — by email, in person, or over WhatsApp.

::: warning Only complete sessions are paid
A session is eligible for salary calculation only when it has **both a valid
sign-in and a valid sign-out record**. A shift you worked but forgot to sign out
of will not appear until the "Abnormal sign in+out" sheet is filled in and the
record is corrected — see
[Your First Shift](/guide/first-shift#if-something-goes-wrong).
:::

## MPF (Mandatory Provident Fund)

**Every student helper has MPF.** The 16.5-hour cap above does not change that —
MPF has nothing to do with the "468" rule. Under the MPF Schemes Ordinance an
employer must enrol **any** employee aged **18 to 64** who has been employed for
**60 days or more**, full-time or part-time, however few hours a week they work.

Lingnan University's scheme is the **Fidelity Retirement Master Trust**
(富達退休集成信託). That is why there is a **Fidelity MPF member enrolment form**
to sign when you sign your contract. Fill it in properly and keep a copy — it is
what opens your MPF account.

### The extra 5%

| Per hour worked | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Your hourly rate | **$65** |
| Employer MPF contribution (5%) | **$3.25** |
| Total cost to the University | $65 x 1.05 = **$68.25** |
| Paid into your bank account | **$65** |
| Paid into your MPF account | **$3.25** |

The 5% is paid **on top of** your salary by the employer — it is not deducted
from your $65. But you do not get it now: it goes straight into your MPF account
and stays locked there until you are allowed to withdraw it.

You normally contribute **nothing** yourself. An employee only starts paying
their own 5% once monthly income reaches the **minimum relevant income level of
$7,100**, and 13 to 16.5 hours a week stays well under that. The employer's 5%
is due either way. (If a month ever does go over $7,100, 5% comes out of your
own pay as well, up to the **$30,000** maximum relevant income level.)

### Where your money is

Since **30 December 2025** the Fidelity Retirement Master Trust has been
administered on the **eMPF Platform**, the MPFA's central system. Register at
[empf.org.hk](https://www.empf.org.hk/) to see your account, your balance and
every contribution the University has made for you. If a month looks missing,
raise it with Paul first, then with the MPFA.

### When can you take the money out

The default is **age 65**. MPF is a retirement scheme, not a savings account —
graduating or leaving the student helper post does not release it. Apart from
reaching 65 there are only six early-withdrawal grounds: early retirement at
**60**, **permanent departure from Hong Kong**, total incapacity, terminal
illness, a **small balance account**, and death.

**Local students.** In practice, leave it alone. Your next Hong Kong job opens a
new account, and you can consolidate the student helper money into it — or into
a personal account with a trustee you choose — so it is not scattered across
schemes. You withdraw at 65, or at 60 if you retire early and declare you do not
intend to work again.

**Non-local students leaving Hong Kong after graduation.** Use the **permanent
departure from Hong Kong** ground. Submit to the eMPF Platform:

1. The **claim form** for payment of accrued benefits on the ground of permanent
   departure.
2. A **statutory declaration** — form **MPF(S)-W(SD2)** — declaring that you have
   left or will leave Hong Kong to reside elsewhere permanently, with no
   intention of returning to live or work here. Swear it before a Commissioner
   for Oaths at a Home Affairs Department office while you are still in Hong
   Kong, or before a notary public once you are overseas.
3. **Documentary proof that you are permitted to reside outside Hong Kong** —
   for example a residence permit or identity document for the place you are
   moving to, settlement papers, a tenancy agreement, an employment contract, a
   utility bill in your name, or a letter of release from the Inland Revenue
   Department.

::: danger Travel documents alone are not enough
A Home Return Permit by itself does not prove you have moved to the Mainland,
and a BN(O) passport by itself does not prove you have moved to the UK. The
trustee weighs all your documents together. And this ground is **once in a
lifetime**: withdraw on it, come back and work in Hong Kong, and you cannot use
it again. A false or misleading declaration is a criminal offence — up to a
**$100,000 fine and 12 months' imprisonment** on a first conviction.
:::

Payment normally follows within **30 days** of the eMPF Platform receiving all
the required documents, so start the paperwork before you fly out if you can.

::: tip Small balance is often the easier route
A student helper's balance is small — 13 hours a week at $65 puts roughly $180 a
month into MPF. If your total is **$5,000 or less**, held in **only one MPF
scheme** with nothing in any other, **12 months** have passed since your last
contribution, and you declare that you do not intend to become employed or
self-employed in Hong Kong in the foreseeable future, you can withdraw on the
**small balance account** ground instead — no proof of overseas residence
needed. Any new MPF contribution restarts the 12 months.
:::
