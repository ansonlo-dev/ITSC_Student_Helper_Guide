# Code of Conduct

As a Student Helper you are acting on behalf of ITSC. Users will treat what you
say as official, so accuracy and professionalism matter.

## Confidentiality

- Anything you see while helping a user — files, emails, grades, personal data —
  is confidential. Do not read it beyond what the task requires, and never share
  or discuss it afterwards.
- Do not take photos or screenshots of a user's screen or data.
- This obligation continues after your employment ends.

## Passwords and accounts

- Never ask a user to tell you their password.
- Never type a password into a system on a user's behalf. Let the user type it,
  and look away while they do.
- Never use a staff or shared account for anything outside your assigned duties.
- Never share your own credentials with anyone, including other helpers.

## Scope of work

Do only what you have been trained and authorised to do. Escalate anything
involving:

- Server, network, or security configuration
- Account creation, deletion, or permission changes
- Data recovery from a failing disk
- Anything a user is angry or formally complaining about
- Any suspected security incident or policy violation

## Professional behaviour

- Be punctual. If you will be late, tell your supervisor.
- Be patient and respectful, regardless of how technical the user is.
- No eating at the service counter; keep phone use out of sight while on duty.
- Treat every user equally — students, staff, visitors, and fellow helpers.

## Reporting problems

Report immediately to a full-time staff member if you encounter:

- A suspected data breach, malware infection, or phishing campaign
- Damaged, missing, or stolen equipment
- Any behaviour from a user that makes you feel unsafe or harassed

You will never be blamed for reporting something in good faith.
