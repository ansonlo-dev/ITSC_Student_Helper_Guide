# Work Precautions

## Academics first

**Academics first. Enjoy your university life.**

This job is built around your studies, not the other way round. Book the shifts
you can genuinely make, and drop them early when your studies need the time.

## The expectations

::: tip What the team asks of you
**Honesty · Self-discipline · Responsibility · Time management · Communication ·
Respect each other**
:::

Academics always come first — but while you are on duty, ITSC student helpers
are expected to show the highest standard of each of those. Manage your own
schedule and academic workload so that you can still fully enjoy your university
life.

## Why the programme exists

::: tip More than a pay cheque
As Paul puts it, the Student Helper programme is designed not just for earning
money, but as an opportunity to improve your **communication**,
**problem-solving** and **career readiness**. Have self-discipline, respect your
peers — and above all, enjoy your university life.
:::

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Be **honest** | **Take or leave a shift without sending a message** |
| Be **punctual** | Be **absent without leave** |
| Be **helpful** | Send **leave messages too late** |
| Be **polite** | **Submit a report without performing the check** |
| **Check the WhatsApp group at least once a day** | **Sign in late or sign out early** |

::: danger Never submit a report you did not earn
Submitting a checking result without doing the check is the one thing that
destroys the value of the whole system — the report is the only signal ITSC has
that a room is ready.
:::

Signing out even 15 minutes early is treated as a breach, not a rounding error.

## Work evidence

Checking duties ask you to upload a **photo** as work evidence.

| Duty | Photo field |
| --- | --- |
| Morning Check | **Mandatory** |
| Lecture Room Check | Present (Work-Evidence, question 26) |
| Computer Lab Check | Optional |
| Printer Check | Optional |

::: warning What a valid photo looks like
It must show the **time** and the **surrounding environment** — not just a
selfie. A good pattern: photograph the camera while it is switched on, yourself,
and the state of the room.
:::

The requirement was introduced after a helper signed in and left for breakfast.
ITSC can read login records anyway; the photo is asked for as simple practical
proof, and the rest of the form is still trusted without one.

## Wear your badge

::: danger Mandatory dress code and identification
Student helpers must wear the helper badge **while on duty, at all times**.
:::

The badge does three jobs:

1. It establishes a **professional image**.
2. It **identifies you** to staff and to the students you are helping.
3. It lets **security guards identify you** when you ask for room access
   after hours.

::: warning No badge, no shift
Collect your badge before your first duty. Working without one means the hours
**are not counted**, and repeated cases can have your duties suspended
altogether. If you have lost yours or cannot collect it — you are on exchange,
or away from Hong Kong — tell Jason rather than working without it.
:::

## Taking leave

If you cannot make a booked shift:

1. **Post in the WhatsApp group**, in this format:

   ```
   Leave
   Name: Homan
   Date: 15/1/2025
   Time: 10:30-12:30
   Duty: printer check
   Reason: sickness
   ```

2. **Remove your duty record** from
   [helperduty.top](https://helperduty.top) yourself.

Both steps are required — the message alone does not free the slot.

::: warning At least 30 minutes before the shift
That is the floor, not the target. Send it as early as you know, so somebody
else has a real chance to pick the slot up — a leave message posted after the
duty has already started frees nothing and helps nobody.
:::

## Counter tidiness

We need to put everything back to its position, neat and tidy.

- Chairs back to their position after seated.
- Badges back to their cabinet.
- Leave the counter workstation and phone as you found them.

![The badge drawer at the counter](/images/badge-drawer.jpg)
*Badges go back in the cabinet, not in your bag*

## Leaving a room after a check

- **Shut down every PC** you checked.
- **Shut down the projector and visualizer**, and turn off the control panel.
- **Leave one light on.**

## Returning keys and materials

- Return the **Ricoh printer key** to where you picked it up, and lock the
  printer again.
- Return the duty bag and its contents (badge, mic, covers, audio CD, batteries)
  to LCH206A or MB202 as appropriate. **Put everything back inside it first** —
  a CD left on a lectern and a bag left in the room after a check have both
  happened, and the next helper is the one who pays for it.
- If you used the last of something in the bag — the microphone hygiene covers,
  for example — **take a different bag and say so in the group**, so it gets
  restocked.
- Loaned equipment must go back to the store room, complete with all accessories.
