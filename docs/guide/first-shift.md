# Your First Shift

## Before you arrive

- Confirm your shift time and location on the duty roster.
- Know who your supervisor on duty is.
- Bring your student card — you may need it for building or room access.
- Dress tidily. You are the first person many users meet at ITSC.

## Arriving

::: tip Start-of-shift checklist
1. Sign in on the duty log.
2. Read the handover notes from the previous shift.
3. Check the service counter / lab for anything left broken or unresolved.
4. Log in to the tools you need for the shift.
5. Ask the previous helper if anything is still outstanding.
:::

## During the shift

- Greet every user who approaches. Even a "I'll be with you in a moment" is better
  than silence.
- Log every request you handle — see [Service Counter](/duties/service-counter).
- If you cannot solve something within a reasonable time, escalate rather than
  keep the user waiting.

## Ending the shift

::: tip End-of-shift checklist
1. Finish or hand over anything still in progress.
2. Write handover notes for the next helper — be specific about unresolved cases.
3. Tidy the counter / lab: chairs, cables, loan equipment returned to place.
4. Log out of all shared accounts and lock the workstation.
5. Sign out on the duty log.
:::

## If you cannot make a shift

Tell your supervisor as early as possible and follow the team's swap procedure.
Do not simply arrange a replacement between yourselves without informing staff.
