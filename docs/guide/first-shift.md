# Your First Shift

Every duty follows the same five steps, whatever you are rostered on.

## The work flow

1. **Reserve the duty yourself** — [helperduty.top](https://helperduty.top)
2. **Sign in when you are on duty** — [ics.ln.edu.hk/helper](https://ics.ln.edu.hk/helper)
3. **Perform the duty** — counter, printer check, lab check, etc.
4. **Submit the checking result** — the Microsoft Forms checklist for that duty
5. **Sign out** — [ics.ln.edu.hk/helper](https://ics.ln.edu.hk/helper)

::: warning The steps are enforced by the system
You cannot sign in without a duty booking, and you cannot sign out without
having submitted the checking result. Skipping step 1 or step 4 will block you.
:::

## 1. Duty Booking System

**[helperduty.top](https://helperduty.top)**

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Username | Your CNA (your student email address) |
| Password | Your **Student ID** |

::: warning On campus, or on LUVPN
The booking system is reachable **only from the campus Wi-Fi network**, or
through **LUVPN** when you are off campus.
:::

::: danger Keep the system inside the team
It is a **private, standalone database** — not linked to the main LU systems,
which is why the password is your Student ID and not your LU password.
**Do not disclose that it exists to other university departments** (OSA, for
example).
:::

The calendar has Day / Week / Month views. Each column is a duty slot —
ITSC Service Counter, LU Campus Printer, Computer Lab Helper 1–4, Lecture Room
Helper 1–2, Computer Lab and Lecture, and Hostel B / H / WJY Helper.

- **To book** — go to the date, then click the empty cell in the duty column and
  time row you want.
- **To delete a booking** — open your own booking in the calendar and remove it.
  Do this yourself whenever you take leave.

### Booking rules

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Counter shifts** | Can be booked in blocks as short as **30 minutes** |
| **Maintenance checks** | Printer, computer lab, lecture room and morning checks must be booked for the **entire time slot** |
| **Weekly limit** | The system enforces a weekly maximum — see [Work Hours & Salary](/guide/schedule-and-pay#work-hours) |
| **Overlaps** | Overlapping bookings are **blocked outright** |

Only Paul can override the work limit, and only for a specific reason.

### Leave and swaps

1. Send a detailed **leave message on WhatsApp** — name, date, time, duty and
   reason.
2. **Find a substitute** to cover it. Cover is **first come, first served**.
3. **Delete your booked slot yourself** in the booking system.

The full wording and examples are on
[Work Precautions](/guide/code-of-conduct#taking-leave).

## 5. Sign out

If you try to sign out before submitting, the page shows your outstanding work
in red, for example:

> **Missing the following work reports:**
> \*\*\* Printer Check \*\*\* (expected: 1, actual: 0)

![Sign out blocked by a missing work report](/images/signout-blocked.jpg)
*Sign out is blocked until the checking result is in*

Once the result is in, the page shows **Duty Completed** and the
**Sign Out (Desktop/AV)** button becomes available.

![Duty Completed with the Sign Out button available](/images/signout-completed.jpg)
*Duty Completed — now you can sign out*

### Before you can sign out

1. **Submit the duty's Microsoft Form.**
2. **Wait for the CC'd copy to arrive in your student email** — that receipt is
   your proof the submission went through.
3. Then press **Sign Out (Desktop/AV)**.

::: danger Always submit from your own account
Submit every form **from your own account**. A form sent under someone else's
name does not release your sign-out, and it credits the check to the wrong
person.
:::

::: warning Sign out exactly on time
Finished a 2-hour check in 30 minutes? You may submit the form straight away,
but you must **wait and sign out exactly on time**. IP addresses are not tracked
— but every early and late sign-out is logged and counts towards your helper
performance statistics.
:::

## If something goes wrong

Forgot to sign in or out, signed in twice, signed out at the wrong time, or
worked overtime? Fill in the **"Abnormal sign in+out"** Excel file, recording:

| Column | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| Date | The date of the problem |
| Problem | e.g. Fail to Sign In / Fail to Sign Out / Sign in twice / Sign out wrong time |
| Name | Your name |
| Responsible Timeslot | e.g. 13:30–16:00 |
| Wrong Time | The time that was actually recorded |
| Correct Time | What it should have been |
| Type | User service / Counter / Hostel Clinic / others |
| Status | Left for the manager to fill in once adjusted |

![The Abnormal sign in and out spreadsheet](/images/abnormal-signin-sheet.jpg)
*The "Abnormal sign in+out" workbook, one sheet per month*

Also let the managers know for anything **more than 15 minutes** early, late, or
overtime.
