## Five habits of a good helper

1. **Book only the shifts you can make.** Academics first — and drop a shift
   early rather than late.
2. **Follow the five steps every time.** Book, sign in, do the duty, submit the
   result, sign out. The system will block you if you skip one.
3. **Ask the duty staff first.** Guessing costs far more time than asking.
4. **Write it down.** Every enquiry goes on the form; every fault goes on the
   checklist, with the room and machine name.
5. **Put everything back.** Keys, badges, chairs, equipment, batteries.
