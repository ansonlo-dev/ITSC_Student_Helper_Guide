## Five habits of a good helper

1. **Reproduce before you fix.** See the problem yourself; user descriptions are
   often a step removed from what actually happened.
2. **Change one thing at a time.** Otherwise you will never know what worked.
3. **Write down what you tried.** For yourself, the next shift, and the user.
4. **Set a time limit.** If you are 15 minutes in with no progress, escalate.
5. **Close the loop.** Tell the user what happened and what to do if it recurs.
