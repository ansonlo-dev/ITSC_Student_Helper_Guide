## ITSC Morning Check 培訓會議概覽
- 主講人為 ITSC 負責人，對象為學生助理（Student Helpers）
- 目的：講解 Morning Check 的流程、Checklist 要求及工作態度期望
- 成果：完成 Morning Check 培訓，歷時約半小時；今日工時計 1.5 小時

## Morning Check 的設計背景與目的
- 原本只有 SEK 兩間房需要 Morning Check，現已擴展至更多房間
- 集中檢查**較新式的房間**（如 LBY 地下、LKK 幾間）
	- 非正式統計：新式房間故障率較高
	- 舊房間會 randomly 抽查
- 目標：確保**第一節上課前**房間一切正常，減少老師致電投訴
	- 理想提交時間：**9:00 前，最遲 9:30 前**
- 每次 Morning Check 限定 **3 間房，2 小時**
	- 平均每間房約 15 分鐘，3 間 = 約 45 分鐘，餘下時間填報告
	- SEK 原有兩間除外，其他全部為 3 間房制

## Checklist 逐項說明
- **房間狀態選項**：
	- 已 Check / In Use / 鎖門
	- 不希望出現「only in use」情況（應早於使用者到達）
- **燈光**：
	- 有壞燈需 report（例如 MBG11 成日跳掣）
- **Manual**：
	- 三份 manual 需確認在場
- **PC**：
	- 確認能開機 **且** 能 Log in（兩者缺一不可）
	- Log in 不到可能因為沒有 network，需即時解決
	- 測試後記得 **Restart**
- **Keyboard**：
	- 至少試 ABCDE 幾個按鍵
	- 檢查有否污糟、按鍵缺失（例：曾有 F4 鍵不見）
- **Mouse**：
	- 左右鍵、滾輪正常，無黏性即可
- **Speaker Sound Test**：
	- 播音頻測試，有聲即 OK；電腦端亦需測試
- **Podium Monitor**：
	- 確認顯示正常
- **Display Equipment（投影機/顯示器）**：
	- 注意顯示模式，避免設定成 **PC Screen Only** 或 **Second Screen Only**
	- 應設為 **Extend** 或 **Duplicate**
	- 右下角顯示設定需留意
- **Microphone Sound Test**：
	- 開麥測試，有聲即 OK
	- 曾發現 LBY、LKKG01 的 mic 無聲，Morning Check 有效提前解決
	- 有 IR mic 的房間需一併測試
- **Camera**：
	- Facing Student / Facing Teacher 兩個方向均需測試
	- MBY201 及新房 camera 與舊款不同
	- 建議用 **Zoom（Video and Effect）** 測試，因 Windows 內置 Camera App 切換時會 freeze
	- LKK 新房 camera 固定在幕牆，無 tripod
- **Tripod**：
	- 確認企穩、托頭正常、朝向學生
	- 部分房間無 tripod，可 mark「No Tripod」
- **Control Panel**：
	- 按鍵有反應、音量可調
	- Control Panel 及 PC 必須先 OK，才能測試 mic 等其他設備
- **Visualizer**：
	- 開啟並切換測試

# ITSC Morning Check Checklist Training Session for Student Helpers
## ITSC Morning Check Training Session Overview
- The presenter is the head of ITSC, and the audience consists of student assistants
- Objective: To explain the Morning Check process, checklist requirements, and expected work attitude
- Results: Completed the Morning Check training, which lasted about half an hour; today’s work hours: 1.5 hours

## The Background and Purpose of Morning Check
- Originally, only two rooms in SEK required a morning check, but this has now been expanded to include more rooms
- Focus the inspection on **newer rooms** (such as those in the basement of LBY and several rooms in LKK)
- Unofficial statistics: The failure rate for the new-style rooms is higher.
- Old rooms will be randomly selected for inspection
- Goal: Ensure that everything is in order in the room **before the first class**, thereby reducing the number of complaints from teachers
- Recommended submission time: **Before 9:00 a.m., no later than 9:30 a.m.**
- Each Morning Check session is limited to **3 rooms for 2 hours**
- About 15 minutes per room on average; 3 rooms = about 45 minutes; the remaining time will be spent filling out the report
- With the exception of the two existing SEK units, all others are three-bedroom units

## Checklist: Item-by-Item Explanation
- **Room Status Options**:
	- Checked In / In Use / Locked
	- We do not want to see the "in use" message (it should appear before the user arrives)
- **Lighting**:
	- Please report any malfunctioning lights (e.g., MBG11 keeps tripping the circuit breaker)
- **Manual**:
	- Please ensure that all three manuals are present
- **PC**:
	- Verify that the system can boot up **and** log in (both are required)
	- If you can't log in, it may be due to a lack of network connection; this needs to be resolved immediately.
	- Remember to **restart** after testing
- **Keyboard**:
	- Try pressing at least the ABCDE keys
	- Check for dirt or missing keys (e.g., the F4 key was once missing)
- **Mouse**:
	- Left and right buttons and scroll wheel should work normally; there should be no sticking.
- **Speaker Sound Test**:
	- Play the audio test; if you hear sound, it’s OK; you must also test this on the computer.
- **Podium Monitor**:
	- Confirm that the display is working properly
- **Display Equipment (Projectors/Monitors)**:
	- Pay attention to the display mode and avoid setting it to **PC Screen Only** or **Second Screen Only**
	- Should be set to **Extend** or **Duplicate**
	- Please note the settings displayed in the lower-right corner
- **Microphone Sound Test**:
	- Test the microphone; if you hear sound, it's working.
	- Previously encountered an issue where the microphones on the LBY and LKKG01 were silent; this was effectively resolved in advance via Morning Check
	- Rooms equipped with IR microphones must also be tested
- **Camera**:
	- Testing is required for both the student-facing and teacher-facing orientations
	- The MBY201 and the new home camera differ from the older models
	- We recommend using **Zoom (Video and Effects)** for testing, as the built-in Windows Camera app may freeze when switching between views.
	- LKK New Home camera mounted on the curtain wall, no tripod
- **Tripod**:
	- Ensure the device is stable, the camera is positioned correctly, and it is facing the students
	- Some rooms do not allow tripods; please mark "No Tripod"
- **Control Panel**:
	- The buttons respond, and the volume is adjustable
	- The Control Panel and PC must be working properly before you can test the microphone and other devices
- **Visualizer**:
	- Open and switch between tests
